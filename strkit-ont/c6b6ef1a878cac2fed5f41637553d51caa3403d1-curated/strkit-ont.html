Installed unmodified upstream STRkit v0.25.0 (`c6b6ef1`) into an isolated Python virtual environment (`pip install .`, zero source modifications) and ran `strkit call` with `--realign --seed 183 --processes 4` against the HG00113 Oxford Nanopore R10 SUP CODIS reference slice (`ont-bam-hg38`, 20 autosomal CODIS loci in 4-column `motif.bed` format) and local `hg38.fa`. Execution completed cleanly in ~4.4 s (exit 0), producing a valid 20-record VCF (`sample.vcf`) with 20/20 called diploid genotypes and 702 total supporting reads (`FORMAT/DP`, mean depth 35.1x).
